# Acme Corp — Company Overview

Acme Corp is a B2B SaaS company that helps marketing teams automate their campaign workflows.
Our flagship product is **Magpie Marketing OS**, an AI-powered platform for copywriting,
SEO, ad management, and email automation.

## Pricing Tiers

- **Starter**: $49/month — up to 3 users, 5,000 words/month, basic templates
- **Professional**: $149/month — up to 10 users, 50,000 words/month, advanced templates, A/B testing
- **Enterprise**: $499/month — unlimited users, 500,000 words/month, custom API, dedicated support

## Brand Voice

- **Tone**: Helpful, witty, direct
- **Do**: Use plain language, be concise, show personality
- **Don't**: Use marketing jargon, be overly formal, make exaggerated claims
- **Style**: Write like a smart friend who knows marketing, not a corporate brochure

## Target Audience

- Marketing managers at B2B SaaS companies (20–200 employees)
- Marketing agencies managing multiple clients
- Freelance copywriters and content marketers

## Key Features

- AI-generated email subject lines and body copy
- Landing page copywriter with conversion templates
- Facebook/LinkedIn/Google ad copy generator
- SEO blog post optimizer with keyword suggestions
- Brand voice enforcement across all output
- PDF and Word export for client presentations

## Competitive Positioning

Unlike generic AI writing tools, Magpie is built specifically for marketing teams.
We understand campaign structure, brand guidelines, and conversion-focused copy.
Our output is ready to publish, not just a first draft.

## Common Objections

- "Is this just another GPT wrapper?" → No, we're built for marketing workflows with brand voice enforcement.
- "Is my data safe?" → Yes, all processing is on-premise or via your chosen API provider.
- "Do I need technical skills?" → No, Magpie works through natural language commands.
