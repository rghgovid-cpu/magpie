# Acme Corp — Brand Voice Guidelines

## Voice Personality

We are the smart friend who knows marketing. Not the corporate suit. Not the hype-beast.
Just someone who gets it and can explain it simply.

## Do

- Use contractions (we're, don't, can't)
- Write short sentences (under 20 words when possible)
- Use active voice
- Address the reader as "you"
- Be specific with numbers and examples
- Show, don't tell (use examples instead of adjectives)

## Don't

- Use phrases like "synergy," "leverage," "paradigm shift," "game-changer"
- Write in passive voice
- Use ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Make claims like "best in class" or "industry leading" without proof
- Write like a brochure

## Examples

### Before (corporate)
> Our enterprise-grade solution leverages synergistic AI capabilities to
> paradigm-shift your marketing workflows.

### After (Acme voice)
> Our AI helps your marketing team write better copy, faster. No jargon required.

## Channel-Specific Tone

- **Email**: Conversational, like writing to a colleague
- **Ads**: Punchy, benefit-first, under character limits
- **Landing pages**: Clear headline, proof, one call to action
- **Social media**: Short, witty, emoji-okay (sparingly)
