# Brand Voice Skill

Use this skill to document or audit a brand's voice so every marketing asset sounds consistent.

## Capture

- Brand personality (3-5 traits, e.g. bold, helpful, witty)
- Tone by channel (social vs email vs support vs ads)
- Do's: preferred words, phrasing, rhythm
- Don'ts: forbidden words, jargon, clichés, banned claims
- Grammar & style choices (Oxford comma, numbers, units)

## Output format

- Voice chart (trait -> how it sounds -> example)
- Channel tone matrix
- Do / don't word list
- Before/after rewrite examples
